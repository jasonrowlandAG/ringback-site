Missed Call SMS Templates for Australian Plumbers (That Sound Like You)

A missed call text only works if the caller believes a real plumber sent it. Generic corporate messages get ignored.

Rules before you copy any template

  1. Identify yourself
  2. Say why you are texting
  3. Give one clear next step
  4. Keep it short
  5. Do not promise a time you cannot keep

On the tools (business hours)

Hi, it is your business name. Sorry I missed you. I am on a job. Reply 1 and I will call you back as soon as my hands are free. Reply STOP to opt out.

After hours

Hi, it is your business name. Just missed your call. I am off the tools for the night. Reply 1 and I will ring you first thing. If it is bursting, say emergency. Reply STOP to opt out.

What not to write

  • Your call is important to us
  • Please leave a detailed message
  • Emergency rates apply after 5pm in the first text
  • Long paragraphs

Price and after-hours rates belong in the callback, not the recovery text.

Bottom line

The best missed call SMS for a Sydney plumber is short, honest, and written in your voice. Offer one next step. Separate after-hours from on-the-job.
